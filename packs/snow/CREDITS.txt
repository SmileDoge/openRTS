openRTS snow (the openrts-snow pack) / снег openRTS (пак openrts-snow)

Snow masks for the bodies of the ChS7 and the VL10 (VL10u), laid as the
season's or a snowfall's snow cover (docs/pbr-materials.md, "Snow maps";
the MATERIALS folder). Маски снега для кузовов ЧС7 и ВЛ10 (ВЛ10у): снег
на кузове зимой и в снегопад (docs/ru/pbr-materials.md, папка MATERIALS).

Author / автор: DmItRoNe, shipped with openRTS by the author's leave
(с разрешения автора). Made for the MaDaDev ChS7 and VL10 textures; the
textures themselves are not in this pack.

From the author's 4096 x 4096 PNG masks, shrunk to 2048 x 2048 and
compressed to BC7 ACE (openrts-ace to-ace --format bc7). Из авторских
масок PNG 4096 x 4096, уменьшенных до 2048 x 2048 и сжатых в BC7 ACE.

- chs7/: zdsLoco_chs7-6A, zdsLoco_chs7-6B (every mdd_CHS7-... folder)
- vl10/: kuz1, kuz2, kuzovdet (the folders whose name holds "vl10")

Another pack, a mod or a MATERIALS folder in the installation replaces
them: openRTS's own packs are below everything (docs/vfs.md).
